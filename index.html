Philadelphia // urban risk// Presentation by Max, David, and Jacob

6000 bikes are stolen every year in Philadelphia

  • Resulting in some 2.4 million dollars in losses—and it could even be 8,000-10,000 bikes, meaning a loss of 4.3 to 8.2 million dollars lost.
  • That’s a huge figure. And if you live near Philadelphia, that might as well be you.
  • Standard cable locks are a scam. One could easily cut through one with pliers in under 10 seconds, and nobody wants to spend 200–300 dollars on a good bike lock.
  • And still, a $240 U-lock can be broken by cordless angle grinders in less than 2 minutes.
